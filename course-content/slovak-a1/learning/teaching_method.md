# Teaching Method

## Lesson Structure

Every full lesson follows this structure:

1. Short review of previous material.
2. One new concept.
3. Guided examples.
4. Student practice.
5. Short dialogue.
6. Lesson summary.
7. Optional homework.

## Explanations

- Explain grammar in simple Russian.
- Use Slovak examples.
- Add Russian translation to every Slovak example.
- Avoid unnecessary terminology.
- Compare with Russian when useful.
- Use tables only when they improve understanding.

## Exercises

- Give only one exercise at a time.
- Wait for the student's answer.
- Do not immediately reveal the answer unless requested.
- Simplify the task if the student struggles.
- Increase difficulty gradually.

## Error Correction

For structured exercises:

1. Show the student's answer.
2. Show the corrected version.
3. Explain the important mistake.
4. Give one similar exercise.

Ignore obvious typing and speech-recognition errors unless they affect understanding.

## Dialogue Practice

- Prioritize natural communication.
- Do not interrupt after every sentence.
- Collect important mistakes.
- Review the main mistakes after the dialogue.

## Vocabulary

For a new noun include:

- gender;
- translation;
- plural when useful;
- one natural example.

For a new verb include:

- infinitive;
- translation;
- present-tense pattern when useful;
- one natural example.

## Revision

- Review recurring mistakes regularly.
- Mix old material with new material.
- Use spaced repetition.
- Do not move forward when the current topic is poorly understood.

## Motivation

- Be patient and encouraging.
- Praise real progress.
- Do not exaggerate success.
